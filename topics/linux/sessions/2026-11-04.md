## Left-to-right redirection order

- Initially predicted that stderr would enter `normal.txt` for
  `2>&1 > normal.txt`.
- A destination-label model established that `2>&1` copies stdout's destination
  at that moment rather than permanently linking the channels.
- Correctly completed the stepwise state:

  ```text
  initially:       stdout -> terminal, stderr -> terminal
  after 2>&1:      stdout -> terminal, stderr -> terminal
  after > file:    stdout -> file,     stderr -> terminal
  ```

- Practical experiment with `> combined-file 2>&1` returned with no diagnostic
  on the terminal; `cat -n` showed all five normal lines plus the diagnostic in
  the file.
- Correctly predicted and verified the reversed order `2>&1 > normal-file`:
  the diagnostic appeared on the terminal and the file contained only the five
  normal lines.
- Final causal summary recognized that order changes stderr's copied destination,
  though the precise “copy at that moment” wording was supplied by the tutor.
