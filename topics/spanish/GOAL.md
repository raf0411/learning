# GOAL

Reach practical CEFR A2 Spanish from a complete-beginner starting point.

## Target abilities

- Start, maintain, repair, and close a short conversation with a Spanish speaker on familiar topics.
- Understand the main point of clear everyday conversations about identity, routines, interests, food, travel, plans, and recent events.
- Ask and answer simple follow-up questions rather than relying on memorized monologues.
- Understand the result, attitude, main reason, and next-step plan in short, predictable Spanish football interviews when the context is known.

## Honest scope

At A2, native speakers may still need to speak clearly, slow down, or repeat themselves. Understanding unrestricted, fast, idiomatic football interviews is a later B1-oriented goal.

## Completion evidence

- Hold a five-minute supported conversation covering introductions, interests, routine, a recent event, and a future plan.
- Complete representative DELE A2 listening and speaking tasks at a consistent passing level.
- Extract the topic, result, attitude, reason, and future plan from several short football-interview clips without a transcript.
