# RESOURCES

## Standards and curriculum

- [Council of Europe — CEFR Companion Volume (2020)](https://rm.coe.int/cefr-companion-volume-with-new-descriptors-2020/16809ea0d4) — authoritative proficiency descriptors.
- [Instituto Cervantes — A1/A2 general objectives](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/01_objetivos_relacion_a1-a2.htm) — Spanish-specific communicative outcomes.
- [Instituto Cervantes — A1/A2 communicative functions](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/05_funciones_inventario_a1-a2.htm) — greetings, information, preferences, plans, and conversation management.
- [Instituto Cervantes — A1/A2 grammar](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/02_gramatica_inventario_a1-a2.htm) — grammatical scope.
- [Instituto Cervantes — A1/A2 pronunciation](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/03_pronunciacion_inventario_a1-a2.htm) — pronunciation and prosody scope.
- [RAE/ASALE — Gramática básica](https://www.rae.es/gram%C3%A1tica-b%C3%A1sica) — authoritative grammar reference.

## Assessment and authentic listening

- [Instituto Cervantes — official DELE preparation materials](https://examenes.cervantes.es/es/dele/preparar-prueba) — official A1/A2 sample tasks, audio, transcripts, and answer keys.
- [LaLiga — broadcast and flash-interview specification](https://assets.laliga.com/assets/2025/11/07/originals/176430d21ac2f66da274d8f0dbaf1dd8.pdf) — source for the predictable structure of post-match interviews.

Resources will be added only when they are actually used in lessons or assessment.
