## Concepts covered

- `active (exited)` describes a service unit with no continuing main process;
  it does not mean an exited daemon is still running.
- A finite `sleep` exits naturally when its duration ends. Its `Type=exec`
  transient service then becomes inactive, and `--collect` allows systemd to
  unload the unit.
- Signal delivery and signal outcome are separate. A signal disposition may
  specify a default action, a registered handler, or ignoring the signal. This
  foundation was introduced but did not yet land.
- The next lesson will separate running/stopped/terminated state from
  foreground/background placement, then cover safe termination and process
  inspection.
