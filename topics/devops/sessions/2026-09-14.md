## Shared-memory retrieval and explanation

After the lab, the tutor asked whether two processes showing RSS 6 MiB each
necessarily occupy 12 MiB of distinct physical RAM. The learner said they did
not know, guessed "no," and reasoned that the processes use different RSS
channels of physical RAM. The conclusion alone did not establish understanding;
RSS is a measurement, and the missing issue is overlap in physical pages.

The tutor explained a simplified model with 2 MiB private to A, one shared
4 MiB block, and 2 MiB private to B. Each process counts 6 MiB in RSS, while
the three distinct blocks total 8 MiB. Summing the two RSS values counts the
same shared 4 MiB twice. This was a teaching example, not measured sharing
on the server. No live sharing experiment was performed.

The final question was not answered before the learner ended the session:
