## Swap direction retrieval

Given mapped pages A B C D with A B C resident, the learner correctly removed
B from the resident line after it left RAM for swap and kept all mappings. They
nevertheless said RES increases. The tutor pointed out that this contradicted
the drawn resident set and repaired the direction: RAM to swap lowers RES;
swap to RAM raises RES; VIRT remains fixed when the mapping remains.

On the immediate reverse-direction check, the learner correctly restored B to
the resident set, increased RES, and held VIRT fixed. This is successful guided
repair, not independent or retained evidence.

## Free and available RAM

Fresh read-only output was:

```text
               total        used        free      shared  buff/cache   available
Mem:           7.2Gi       1.2Gi       4.7Gi        19Mi       1.6Gi       6.0Gi
Swap:          4.0Gi          0B       4.0Gi
```

The learner correctly said the system was not currently short of reusable RAM
but did not know why available exceeded free. Teaching established:

$$
M_{\text{available}} \approx M_{\text{free}} + M_{\text{reclaimable}}
$$

The learner repeatedly described capacity as coming "from available" before
the tutor clarified that available is an estimate, not a separate pool. With a
sentence scaffold, they correctly completed the source as unused RAM plus
occupied reusable cache. This remains GUIDED; independent wording is due after
spacing.

## Shared RSS and SHR

In a two-process example with 2 MiB and 3 MiB private blocks plus one shared
4 MiB block, the learner initially reported only the private amounts as RSS.
They did independently count the distinct physical total as 9 MiB. Teaching
re-established that each process's RSS includes the same shared resident block.

In the immediate fresh example with 1 MiB and 2 MiB private blocks plus one
shared 5 MiB block, the learner correctly calculated RSS values of 6 MiB and
7 MiB, a summed RSS of 13 MiB, and a distinct physical total of 8 MiB.

On an unfamiliar written `top` row, the learner correctly said that 12 MiB SHR
was inside 30 MiB RES and represented potentially shareable memory rather than
proof another process currently shared all 12 MiB. They initially supplied only
the labels for VIRT and RES, so their operational definitions were retaught.
A later incorrect 5 MiB transition treated the question as a fresh scenario;
once the continued starting row was explicit, the learner correctly changed
VIRT/RES/SHR from 100/35/12 to 100/32/12 when 3 MiB left RAM while remaining
mapped. This supports a context-reading mistake rather than another direction
reversal in that final exchange.
